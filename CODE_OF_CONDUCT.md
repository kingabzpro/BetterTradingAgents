# Code of conduct

Be decent. This is a small open-source project about stock research and paper trading, and it should stay a place where people can ask questions, disagree, and be wrong in peace.

Concretely:

- Argue with ideas, not people. No insults, trolling, or personal attacks.
- No harassment of any kind, in issues, pull requests, reviews, or anywhere else in this project's spaces.
- Do not publish other people's private information.
- Sexualized language and hate speech are out. A stranger should be able to read any thread here.

If something crosses the line, report it to [@kingabzpro](https://github.com/kingabzpro) (GitHub message) or via [abid.work](https://abid.work). Reports are treated as confidential. As the maintainer, I may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block accounts over conduct; serious or repeated problems end in a ban.

These rules cover all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, the wiki) and anywhere you are representing the project.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
